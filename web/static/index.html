2. Mode

Motion Transfer rebuilds the whole shot. Object Swap changes one element and keeps the rest of the shot intact.

3. Swap mask · white = replace

A mask image over the thing to replace. White pixels are regenerated; black pixels are kept from the source. One image is reused across every frame.
